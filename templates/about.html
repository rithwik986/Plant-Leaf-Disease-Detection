About Plant Leaf Disease Detection

A simple and effective system to detect plant leaf diseases and provide preventive measures for healthy crop growth.

Our Mission

We aim to support farmers, gardeners, and students with a reliable tool that detects plant leaf diseases at an early stage. Our goal is to reduce crop loss, improve yield, and encourage sustainable farming practices.

Why Choose This System?

  • Detects common plant leaf diseases with accuracy
  • Provides treatment and preventive suggestions
  • Works with different crops and plant species
  • Easy-to-use interface for everyone
  • Encourages sustainable and healthy farming methods

Features

Disease Detection

Identify diseases from leaf images or by selecting symptoms from a database for faster diagnosis.

Treatment Suggestions

Get helpful recommendations including remedies, fertilizers, and supplements to restore plant health.

Preventive Guidance

Follow preventive tips to stop disease spread and maintain stronger, healthier crops.
